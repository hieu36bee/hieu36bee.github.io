Privacy Policy

Soi Kenh · last updated 18 October 2026

Soi Kenh ("the app") is a private Windows desktop application used by its owner and their staff to analyse YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses when a user connects their YouTube channel with Google sign-in, and how that data is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Analytics API, YouTube Reporting API and YouTube Data API). By connecting a channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

Only after you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app requests these read-only permissions:

  • youtube.readonly – the name and ID of the channel you connected, and titles of its videos;
  • yt-analytics.readonly – channel and video statistics: views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, audience retention, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate;
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly (optional) – estimated revenue.

The app does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and it cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

The data is shown inside the app to the channel owner and their team (tables, charts, reports) and may be summarised by an AI service to suggest content improvements. Only aggregated statistics and video titles are sent for that summary — never your Google credentials or access tokens.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored locally on the computer where the app is installed. The Google access permission (refresh token) is encrypted with Windows data protection on that computer. The app has no server that collects your YouTube data. The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Click "Ngắt kết nối" (Disconnect) in the app: the app revokes its Google permission and deletes all stored data of that channel from the computer.
  • You can also revoke access at any time on your Google Account page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revoking, the app can no longer read new data.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
