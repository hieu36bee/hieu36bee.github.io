Soi Kenh

A private desktop tool for analysing our own YouTube channels.

Soi Kenh is a Windows desktop application used internally by the channel owner and their team. It tracks public statistics of YouTube channels, and — when a channel owner explicitly connects their own channel with Google sign-in — shows that channel's private YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate, estimated revenue) so the team can decide what videos to make and how to improve them.

  • Access is read-only: the app cannot upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube.
  • Data stays on the computer where the app runs. It is not sold or shared.
  • The app is not offered to the public; it is used by its owner and staff only.
